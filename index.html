WHO WE ARE

Service with purpose. Leadership with courage. Impact with evidence.

Youth Volunteer of RP Gishari College is a student-led community for volunteering, learning, leadership, innovation and practical service. We document the work honestly so that youth action can be seen, learned from and multiplied.
